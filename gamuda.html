Where it began

Project Background

Gamuda Land (GL) is a digital platform built to give property residents a more connected and convenient experience. It brings a range of services into one ecosystem, so residents can access information, manage their activities, and engage with their community more easily.

As the platform grew, more services and more user needs made it harder to keep the experience clear, consistent, and easy to navigate.

The project focused on refining user flows, simplifying interactions, and establishing a more consistent visual and design foundation across the platform.

The hand that draws

My Role

As a UI/UX Designer, I worked on different parts of the GL platform, from exploring user flows and ideas to designing and refining the final UI. I collaborated closely with the BA, PM, design, and development teams throughout the process, and presented my designs to the client to gather feedback and make sure the solutions aligned with their needs.

The Challenge

The Outcome

A connected experience across platforms.

GL brought the Resident App, Admin Platform, and Tab Guard App into one connected ecosystem. Each platform was designed around the needs of its users while keeping the experience consistent across touchpoints.

The final designs covered a wide range of resident and property workflows, from accessing services and submitting requests to managing activities and responding to them on-site.

Regular discussions, design presentations, and feedback sessions with the BA, PM, design, development, and client teams helped shape the solutions and keep every platform aligned.

Conclusion

Beyond a single screen, toward one experience

Working on GL gave me the chance to think beyond individual interfaces and understand how different products can work together as one experience.

It strengthened how I work across teams, communicate design decisions, present to clients, and iterate on feedback. Most of all, it showed me how much consistency and clear communication matter when designing for different users, platforms, and workflows within the same ecosystem.
